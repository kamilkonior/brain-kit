# Fidelity

What is delivered is what was agreed, and a departure from it is settled with the user before it is made.

*Falls short:* something near what was agreed is delivered, or something more. *Overshoots:* what was agreed is followed to the letter after the work has shown it to be wrong.

Why: the user agreed to a particular thing, and a quiet departure gives them something they did not approve and may not notice.

**Desirable**

- **Traceability:** each thing delivered goes back to something agreed, and each thing agreed to something delivered.
- **Change control:** a departure from what was agreed goes to the user before it is made.
- **As-built:** where what was delivered differs from what was agreed, the difference is on record.

**Undesirable**

- **Drift:** what is delivered is near what was agreed, and is not it.
- **Gold plating:** extras that nobody asked for are built in.
- **Cutting corners:** the hard part is dropped or thinned without a word.
- **Prototype in production:** something made as a trial is kept as the real thing.
- **Retcon:** what was agreed is rewritten afterwards to fit what was delivered.
