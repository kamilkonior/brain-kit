# Synthesis

The findings join into one picture: a central idea, its parts, and what passes between them.

*Falls short:* a heap of findings that are each true. *Overshoots:* a model larger or tidier than what is there.

Why: findings that make no whole leave the user unable to reason about the thing.

**Desirable**

- **Mental model:** the user gets a picture they can reason with: what the thing is and by what means it works.
- **Carved at the joints:** the parts are the ones the thing really has, each named by what it is responsible for, with what passes between them.
- **Invariants:** what the thing relies on always being so is in the picture.
- **Warts and all:** the special case, the workaround and the leftover are in the picture, at their place.

**Undesirable**

- **Laundry list:** findings stand side by side, with nothing saying how they connect.
- **Architecture astronaut:** layers and patterns are drawn up for what is three functions.
