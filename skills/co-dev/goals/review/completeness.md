# Review: Completeness

The findings speak for the whole of what was to be reviewed: every part of the work in the extent was read, everything the frame says to look for was looked for, what was examined and holds is said, and what was not looked at is named.

*Falls short:* a judgment of the whole from a reading of a part. *Overshoots:* a review that goes on to judge everything the work touches.

*Borders:* what the extent is and what the review looks for belongs to framing; how hard a part is looked at for its risk belongs to scrutiny; whether what is said of a part rests on a reading of it belongs to grounding; the order of the findings and the answer belong to weight; where the findings and the open items are written belongs to return.

## Desirable

- **Parts listed:** what the extent holds is listed at the start (from the diff, the table of contents, the plan's steps), so that no part can drop out unnoticed.
- **Every part accounted for:** each file, section or step in the extent is read, or is named as not read, with the reason.
- **Everything looked for:** each thing the frame says to look for was looked for over the whole extent, not only where the reading happened to be.
- **Last part like the first:** the end of the work gets the reading its risk calls for, not the one the reader's remaining patience allows.
- **Dull parts included:** tests, configuration, migrations, generated and deleted files, and documentation in the extent are read, not passed over as "the rest".
- **Whole as well as parts:** besides its parts the work is looked at as one thing: whether the parts fit each other, and together do what the work is for.
- **Standard gone through:** every item of the standard (each decision, each step, each criterion) was held against the work, and an item that was not is named.
- **Where else:** when a fault is found, the rest of the extent is searched for the same fault, and the places are listed.
- **Sound parts said:** what was examined and holds is said in a line, where the user will rely on it, so that "no findings" names what it covers.
- **Not looked at:** the review says what it did not examine and why (outside the extent, could not be run, no access), where a reader would take it to have been examined.
- **Outside the extent noted:** a fault met outside the extent, and not caused by the work, is noted for the user in a line, and neither examined further nor left unsaid.
- **Stop said:** where a finding makes further reading pointless (the approach does not hold), the reading may stop there, and what was left unread is said.
- **Closed threads:** a question raised during the reading is answered or put on the list before the end.
- **Final check:** before the review is called done it is compared with the frame (the extent, what was to be looked for, the question), and you say what was compared and what was not.
- **Bounded by need:** judging stops at the extent; how far the reading goes outward, to see what the work does to what surrounds it, follows what the question needs.

## Undesirable

### Falls short

- **Thinning out:** the first files are read closely and the later ones thinly, and the report does not say so.
- **Part for whole:** "the change looks sound" rests on the three files that were opened.
- **Easy parts read:** the code is reviewed, and the migration, the configuration and the tests are not.
- **One thing only:** the review looked for style, and reports on the work.
- **Parts without the whole:** each step of the plan is fine, and nobody asked whether together they reach the end state.
- **Instance, not reach:** one place with the fault is reported, and the four others were not looked for.
- **Silent omission:** something was not read and is not mentioned.
- **Silent stop:** a finding that is in the way ends the reading, and the rest of the work goes unread without a word.
- **Premature "done":** the review is declared finished because the session ran long.

### Overshoots

- **Spreading:** the review of a change becomes a review of the module, and then of the codebase.
- **Old faults collected:** faults that were there before the change are listed against it.
- **Every thread pulled:** each oddity outside the extent gets an examination of its own.
- **Nothing may stay open:** the review is held back for a question that changes no decision.
- **Moving finish line:** the extent grows as the reading goes on.
- **Whole again every round:** unchanged parts are read again in full at each later round.

### Counterfeits (looks met, is not)

- **Pass without extent:** "looks good" is given, and what it covers is not said.
- **Mention as coverage:** "there may be similar cases elsewhere" is written, and none was looked for.
- **Coverage by count:** "all forty files reviewed" is offered as the coverage, and nothing says what was looked for in them.
- **Catch-all clause:** "and other minor issues" stands in for findings nobody wrote down.
- **Claimed check:** "I went through everything" is said without the comparison with the extent having been made.
