# Learn: Selection

Of all that the work showed, the few things are taken up that would make the next piece of work go differently: what cost most, what surprised, what came back, and what went well for a reason that can be kept; the rest is named and left.

*Falls short:* the loudest incident taken up, and what cost more never looked at. *Overshoots:* a lesson drawn from everything that happened.

*Borders:* which piece of work is looked over, and whether one thing or the whole, belongs to framing; reading the record belongs to grounding; why a thing happened belongs to cause; whether a change pays belongs to worth, and selection says only which things are worth asking that of; who has the last word on what is taken up belongs to agency; telling the user what was left belongs to return.

## Desirable

- **Looked over whole:** where the frame is a whole piece of work, its record is gone through from start to end before anything is picked, not only its last hour.
- **Candidates listed:** what stood out is listed in a line each, with the place in the record where it shows, before any of it is gone into.
- **By what it cost:** each candidate says what it cost (time, a step done twice, the user's attention, damage), and the costly ones come first.
- **By what will return:** a thing is taken up when the situation that produced it will come again in the work the frame names.
- **Surprises:** what went otherwise than either of you expected is among the candidates, also where it cost nothing this time.
- **What came back:** a thing that happened before, in this work or an earlier one, counts for more than its cost this once, and the record is searched for the earlier cases.
- **Corrections read:** each time the user corrected you, redirected the work or said something a second time is treated as a candidate.
- **Near misses:** what went well only by luck, or because the user caught it, is taken as seriously as what went wrong.
- **What went well:** what went better than usual is among the candidates, where something in the way of working made it so and could be lost.
- **Friction:** what merely slowed the work each time (a question asked over and over, a command looked up again) is counted over the whole piece of work.
- **Both lists:** the user's candidates are asked for and stand beside yours, and where the two lists differ, that is looked at.
- **Few taken:** a handful at most is taken up, in the order of what each would change, and one is enough.
- **One-off let go:** what will not return (an outage, a typing slip, a tool's bad day) is named as that and left.
- **Rest named:** what was seen and not taken up is named in a line each, so that leaving it is a decision.
- **Nothing found said:** where the work shows nothing worth a change, that is said, and is a result.
- **Proportion:** where the frame is one event, that event is the selection.

## Undesirable

### Falls short

- **Loudest first:** the error that broke the build is studied, and the day lost to an unclear step is not mentioned.
- **Last hour only:** the look covers what is still in view at the end of the session.
- **Failures only:** what went well is never asked about, and the way of working loses it at the next change.
- **Own list only:** the things you noticed are taken, and what bothered the user was never asked.
- **Luck passed over:** a wrong command that happened to do no harm is not counted.
- **Slow cost unseen:** the same question was asked in every session, and no single asking was large enough to be noticed.
- **Recurrence unchecked:** a thing is taken as new, and the notes of the last project record the same.
- **Easy over costly:** what is simple to turn into a rule is taken up, and what cost most is left because it is hard.

### Overshoots

- **Everything a lesson:** each small slip, detour and question of the work gets an entry.
- **One-off taken up:** a network outage gets a cause and a rule.
- **Long list:** twelve things are taken up, and none is carried through to a change.
- **Inventory:** every candidate is costed and ranked, where two stood out at a glance.
- **Praise round:** everything that went as it should is listed as a success worth keeping.
- **Old cases dug up:** the look goes on into work outside the frame, for more to learn from.

### Counterfeits (looks met, is not)

- **Went well, by rote:** one good thing is added to the list for balance, and nothing about it could be kept.
- **One thing, three names:** three entries describe one event, and it looks as if much had been learned.
- **Picked to suit:** the things taken up are those where the fault lies furthest from you, or furthest from the user.
- **"The main thing":** one item is called the main one, with nothing on what it cost against the others.
- **Rest unnamed:** "nothing else stood out" is said of a record that was not gone through.
- **Event and consequence:** a thing and what followed from it are listed as two.
